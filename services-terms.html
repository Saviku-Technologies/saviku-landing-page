Services Transaction Terms

Version 1 · 23 July 2026

These terms supplement the Platform Terms for lawful services. The accepted scope, milestones, completion date, price, materials, revision limits and completion evidence form the transaction record.

Provider duties

The provider must have the skill and licences required, describe scope and exclusions accurately, perform with reasonable care, communicate material delays, and submit agreed evidence of completion.

Customer duties

The customer must provide timely access, instructions, approvals and materials, avoid expanding scope without recording a change, and raise any material defect before the confirmation or release point.

Milestones and disputes

The customer pays the M-PESA till account displayed on the accepted deal card. A licensed payment partner safeguards the funds through the accepted completion anchor or milestone and a 24-hour protection window before disbursement. A missed deadline, non-performance or material departure from scope must be disputed within that window and may qualify under the Services Refund Policy. Subjective dissatisfaction alone is insufficient where the provider completed the objectively recorded scope.
