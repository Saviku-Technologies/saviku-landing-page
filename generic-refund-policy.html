Other Transactions Refund Policy

Version 1 · 23 July 2026

Before funding

Either party may cancel without a Saviku transaction charge.

Before the completion anchor

The parties may jointly cancel. If the payee has incurred a clearly agreed cost, any split requires documented evidence and must be lawful.

Anchor failure

The payer may dispute non-performance or a material mismatch before release. The reviewer uses the exact recorded description, deadline, anchor and evidence standard. A full refund is appropriate for complete non-performance; a reasonable split may apply to conforming partial performance.

After release

No automatic platform refund applies after release, except mandatory legal rights or an upheld exceptional dispute. Saviku may instead direct the parties to the appropriate provider, regulator, court or law-enforcement channel.
