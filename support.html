Support

For account, booking, verification, payment or privacy help, contact support@saviku.co.

Include enough detail

Tell us the WhatsApp number associated with your account, the booking or transaction code, and what happened. Never email your password, one-time code, full payment PIN, or an unredacted identity document.

Urgent payment or safety issue

Use the dispute option in Savi before a transaction releases, where available. If you face immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Saviku is not an emergency service.

Privacy request

See the data-deletion and rights request instructions. We may verify your identity before acting on a request.
