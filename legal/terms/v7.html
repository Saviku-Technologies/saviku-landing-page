Saviku Platform Terms

Effective 1 November 2026.

Saviku is a property-management and booking-automation platform, not a party to the underlying accommodation contract. For Saviku-paid bookings, the payment service provider identified in the payment flow processes the collection and transfer. The guest payment is allocated to the recorded booking in Saviku's merchant account with that provider; it is not paid directly to the host at collection. Saviku instructs the provider to release, refund or split the payment only after the applicable booking decision and provider-availability checks.

The ordinary release decision for a new stay is scheduled for 48 hours after the scheduled check-in time if no eligible cancellation or dispute is open and no review hold is on the booking. Provider settlement is normally expected within two business days after payment, but that timing is an estimate rather than a promise and is not the booking release trigger. Provider, mobile-network, compliance, or banking delays may extend it. A displayed release decision does not mean a recipient has been paid; payment is final only after the provider confirms the transfer.

Users must provide accurate information, use the platform lawfully, and cooperate with identity, KYC, AML, dispute, and payment-reconciliation checks. Saviku never changes a payout destination from a verified number based only on free text or a caller-supplied name.

Saviku is not responsible for property condition, personal injury, user conduct, force majeure, or other matters outside booking automation and the accepted payment-protection process. Remedies are limited to the accepted policy and applicable law.

Refund grounds and review. While the payment is still held, Saviku's reviewer has the final say on cancellation and dispute outcomes for Saviku-paid bookings. A guest is entitled to a full refund of the protected hold, without the host's permission, where the listing was false or misleading or a promised amenity or item is missing (not as described), where there was no check-in possible, where the stay was not delivered (stay not delivered), where there is a safety concern, or where the guest cancelled inside the free cancellation window. Where a complaint is borderline between these terms and a host's own house rules or policy, the reviewer mediates, checks the request is made in good faith, and may decide in either direction. Hosts agree to this when they accept these terms. The grounds are set out in full in the booking's payment protection terms and refund policy.

Nights stayed on a late cancellation. While the payment is still held, whether a reviewer may charge the nights stayed is decided by when the guest's cancellation or dispute request was recorded: the dispute's creation time, or the cancellation request time (the earlier when both exist). It is not when a reviewer opens the review. The cutoff is midnight at the start of the check-in day, in the property's local time. Nairobi time is used when the property records no other zone. A request that lands after that midnight may be charged for the nights stayed. That includes one night when it lands on the check-in day. A request that lands before that midnight is not reclassified because the review is opened later.

After funds are released to the host, refunds are subject to the host's terms. Saviku does not refund or recover that payment. If the guest contacts Saviku ops after release, Saviku directs them to the host. If the host refuses, Saviku says so and encourages the guest to contact the host.

First guest check. On the first stay at a listing paid through Saviku, Saviku asks the guest to confirm the address of the stay. If the guest has not confirmed by 6 hours before the scheduled release, or says something is wrong, release pauses and only a Saviku reviewer can release the payment. The same check applies to the next qualifying stay if the first ended in an upheld dispute with a refund. Hosts agree that their payout may wait for this review.

Account restriction. After a dispute shows a breach of these terms, Saviku may restrict an account, take down its listings, hold payments not yet released, and refuse new bookings or payouts linked to it. To stop a restricted person returning, Saviku keeps keyed hashes of the identifiers on the account. It does not keep the identifiers in that record. Guests with a booking on a restricted account are looked after under the booking's payment protection terms. To appeal, email support@saviku.co from your registered email. A second reviewer decides, once.

Payment is held under these terms as Saviku's protected hold. The mobile-money service a guest pays with does not protect the booking and is not a party to a review decision.
