Stays Transaction Terms

Version 3 · 28 September 2026

These terms supplement the Platform Terms for accommodation bookings. The booking card, dates, occupancy, price, fees, deposit, host cancellation terms and accepted version form the deal record.

Host duties

The host must have authority to offer the stay; provide accurate location, availability, photos, amenities, rules, price and material limitations; maintain a safe and lawful property; and honour confirmed access. Saviku verification does not transfer those duties to Saviku.

Guest duties

The guest must provide accurate booking and occupant information, pay through the presented route, follow lawful property rules, avoid unauthorised occupants or events, and promptly report a material mismatch with supporting evidence.

Payment processing and allocation

Saviku records the booking and collects payment through the payment service provider identified in the payment flow. The payment is allocated to the booking in Saviku’s merchant account with that provider and is not paid directly to the host at collection.

48-Hour Booking Protection

For a new stay, the ordinary release decision is scheduled for 48 hours after the scheduled check-in time if no eligible dispute or cancellation is open. Provider availability is separate from the release decision. Settlement is normally expected within two business days after payment, but provider or network delays may extend it. No payout is sent until Saviku has provider evidence that the booking’s funds are available.

Disputes

A timely cancellation or dispute pauses release. The guest should report failed access or a material mismatch promptly and provide supporting evidence. A Saviku representative reviews each claim against the accepted booking record and the Stays Refund Policy. Available outcomes may include release, refund or a reasonable split. A queued or initiated transfer is not described as paid until the provider confirms success.

Safety and scope

Saviku is not the accommodation provider, does not guarantee every listing or inspect every property, and is not responsible for personal injury or conduct at a property except where liability cannot lawfully be excluded.
