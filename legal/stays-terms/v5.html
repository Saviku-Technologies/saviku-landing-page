Stays Transaction Terms

Effective 1 November 2026.

  1. Saviku records the booking and collects payment through the payment service provider identified in the payment flow. The payment is allocated to the booking in Saviku's merchant account with that provider and is not paid directly to the host at collection.
  2. For new stays, the ordinary release decision is scheduled for 48 hours after the scheduled check-in time if no eligible dispute or cancellation is open and no hold under clause 10 is on the booking. The accepted booking snapshot is authoritative.
  3. Provider availability is separate from the release decision. Settlement is normally expected within two business days after payment, but provider or network delays may extend it. No payout is sent until Saviku has provider evidence that that booking's funds are available.
  4. A timely cancellation or dispute pauses release. A Saviku reviewer may approve release, refund, or a split under the accepted refund policy. A queued or initiated transfer is not described as paid until the provider confirms success.
  5. A full approved refund returns the promised amount to the verified payer destination without deducting a provider collection fee. Saviku absorbs any such fee it owes. Partial and post-release outcomes follow the reviewed decision and disclosed fee treatment.
  6. After a host payout is confirmed, a later approved guest remedy may require recovery from the host; Saviku will not create a second automatic payout while an earlier transfer is uncertain.
  7. Refund grounds. Saviku refunds the booking's protected hold in full, with no permission needed from the host, when Saviku's reviewer finds any of these: (a) Not as described: the listing was false or misleading (for example its photos, location or facilities), or an amenity or item the listing promised is missing. (b) No check-in possible: the guest could not check in. (c) Stay not delivered: the stay did not take place as booked. (d) Safety: a safety concern at the property. (e) Free cancellation window: the guest cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled check-in time. A full refund on these grounds does not wait for the host to confirm the complaint.
  8. Borderline disputes. Where a complaint sits between these terms and the host's own house rules or policy, Saviku's reviewer mediates, checks that the request is made in good faith, and has the final say, including the power to overrule either party. Where no ground above applies, the reviewer may charge the nights already used and refund the rest, or decide under the accepted refund policy. The host may be asked for their account, but the decision does not depend on the host's agreement.
  9. The holding of payment under these terms is Saviku's protected hold. The mobile-money service used to pay does not protect the booking and is not a party to a dispute decision.
  10. First guest check. On the first stay at a listing paid through Saviku, Saviku asks the guest to confirm the address of the stay. If the guest has not confirmed by 6 hours before the scheduled release, or says something is wrong, release pauses and only a Saviku reviewer can release the payment. The same check applies to the next qualifying stay if the first ended in an upheld dispute with a refund. The reviewer contacts the guest first, and may then release, refund, or charge the nights used. Saviku may also hold a payment while it reviews an account.
