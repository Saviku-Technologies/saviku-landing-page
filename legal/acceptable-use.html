Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Use Saviku lawfully, honestly and only for transactions you are authorised to make. You must not:

  • impersonate another person, misrepresent ownership, reuse another host’s photos or create deceptive listings;
  • sell or arrange illegal, stolen, counterfeit, unsafe or regulated items without required authority;
  • use Saviku for money laundering, sanctions evasion, cash cycling, unauthorised fundraising or payment processing unrelated to a genuine transaction;
  • harass, exploit, discriminate against, threaten or endanger another person;
  • upload malware, scrape the service, bypass limits, probe security or misuse automated access;
  • submit sensitive information you are not authorised to provide, or publish private identity or payment data;
  • manipulate reviews, risk checks, disputes, prices, calendars or transaction evidence.

Enforcement

We may warn, limit, suspend or close accounts; stop or refuse a transaction; preserve evidence; remove content; or report suspected unlawful conduct. Where practical and safe, we provide a reason and a route to contact support@saviku.co. This policy supplements the Platform Terms.
