Stays Refund Policy

Version 3 · 28 September 2026

EventDefault outcome
Before payment is completedCancel without a Saviku transaction charge.
Guest cancels 24 hours or more before check-inFull transaction refund, subject to any clearly disclosed non-refundable third-party charge.
Guest cancels less than 24 hours before check-inOne-night amount may be payable; the remainder is refunded.
Access fails or stay is materially not as advertisedOpen a dispute promptly and provide evidence. A full or partial refund may be awarded.
After check-in without a material mismatchOne-night amount may be payable; unused remainder may be refunded under the recorded policy.
After release is decided but before a host transfer succeedsAn eligible review may stop unsent transfer legs. Any uncertain transfer must be reconciled before a replacement decision.
After a host transfer succeedsAn approved guest remedy enters the host-recovery process and is not an automatic reuse of funds already paid out.

Refund review. A Saviku representative reviews every guest claim and its supporting evidence against this policy before any full or partial refund is approved. A refund request is not automatically approved.

Refunds go only to the payment number or other destination verified for the originating transaction. Missing or conflicting evidence pauses the refund for manual review. A host-specific cancellation policy displayed and accepted at booking may supplement these defaults but cannot remove mandatory consumer rights. Protection fees described as refundable are returned when the triggering policy says so. Bank or mobile-money processing times are outside Saviku’s direct control.
