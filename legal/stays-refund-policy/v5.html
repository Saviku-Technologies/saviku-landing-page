Stays Refund Policy

Effective 1 November 2026.

  • Before funding: cancellation creates no payment charge.
  • Before the booking's release decision: an eligible cancellation or upheld dispute may result in a full refund, partial refund, or split after deterministic policy calculation and human review.
  • After release is decided but before a transfer succeeds: an eligible review may stop unsent legs; uncertain transfers must be reconciled before any replacement decision.
  • After a host transfer succeeds: an approved guest remedy enters the host-recovery process and is not an automatic reuse of funds already paid out.

Refunds go only to the payment number or other destination verified for the originating transaction. Saviku uses the provider transaction, reference, amount, and available identity evidence and never guesses or redirects the destination from free text. Missing or conflicting evidence pauses the refund for manual review.

Saviku does not deduct a provider collection fee from a full approved refund and absorbs any such fee it owes. Transfer fees and the fee treatment for partial or post-release outcomes are accounted for in the reviewed decision.

Full refund grounds. Before the booking's release decision, Saviku's reviewer refunds the protected hold in full, including the protection fee the guest paid, and does not need the host's permission, for any of these:

  • Not as described: a false or misleading listing, or a promised amenity or item that is missing.
  • No check-in possible.
  • Stay not delivered.
  • Safety concern.
  • Free cancellation window: cancelled at least 24 hours before the scheduled check-in time.

Borderline disputes. Where a complaint sits between Saviku's terms and the host's own house rules or policy, Saviku's reviewer mediates, checks the guest is not taking advantage, and has the final say. The reviewer may refund in full, or charge the nights already used and refund the rest. A partial outcome keeps platform fees: the protection fee is not refunded, and the host fee is the frozen schedule on the nights charged.

The reviewer records the reason for every full refund. After a host transfer succeeds, the host-recovery process above applies.
