Retail Refund Policy

Version 1 · 23 July 2026

Before dispatch

Cancellation is available while the seller has not dispatched or committed an expressly disclosed non-refundable cost.

Non-delivery or missed ship-by date

If the seller misses the recorded ship-by or delivery commitment, the buyer may request a refund unless both parties record a new date.

Material mismatch

The buyer must promptly submit photos, messages and delivery evidence showing that the item is materially different, counterfeit, materially defective without disclosure, or the wrong quantity. A review may require return of the item using a traceable method before refund.

Exclusions

Change of mind, disclosed defects, ordinary wear, buyer damage, failure to follow instructions and off-platform promises ordinarily do not qualify, subject to mandatory consumer rights. Outcomes may be a full refund, partial refund, replacement agreement or release.
