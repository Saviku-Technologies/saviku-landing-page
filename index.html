How it works

Four messages from “hello” to one calendar.

Onboarding is a conversation, not a form. A web page opens only where a bigger screen genuinely helps — a photo, a signature, a date grid.

Try it with your own unit

  1. Say hello to Savi

    Message the Saviku number. Savi asks your name, whether you host or manage for others, and how many units you run. That is the whole sign-up.

    ~60 seconds

  2. Add a unit

    Name it, paste the listing link, and Savi pulls what it can read from the page. Anything it cannot read, it asks for — one field at a time.

    No spreadsheet

  3. Connect your channels

    Link Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia or Vrbo. Reservations and availability changes begin flowing in realtime, with no calendar-feed refresh to wait for.

    Realtime two-way sync

  4. Block once, everywhere

    Send a date range in chat. Saviku pushes it to every connected channel immediately and watches incoming booking events in realtime, so an overlap reaches your phone as an alert instead of reaching a guest as a cancellation.

    Alerts, not surprises

Questions

The short answers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Saviku runs inside WhatsApp on the number you already use. A web page opens only for the few things a bigger screen does better — a document photo, a signature, a date grid — and it takes you straight back to the chat.

Is a bot answering me?

Savi is a fixed script, not a chatbot with opinions. Every prompt is a set message with buttons or numbered options, and your input is matched exactly. Nothing about money or identity is ever generated — unrecognised input just gets the options again.

Which channels can I connect?

Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and Vrbo connect with realtime two-way sync. Calendar feeds may still be used to import busy dates from other sources, but they are a fallback rather than the primary sync path.

What happens if two bookings collide?

Saviku receives connected-channel booking events in realtime and reconciles them against the dates it already holds. An overlap raises an alert to your phone with both stays named, so you decide what happens next.

Who holds the money in an escrow deal?

The guest pays the M-PESA till account shown on the deal card. A licensed payment partner protects the funds for 24 hours before disbursement, while Saviku records the deal, release conditions and any dispute opened within that window. Saviku does not independently hold regulated client funds.

Can agents and property managers use it?

Yes. Agents follow the same verification ladder plus referee confirmations, and get a shareable profile that shows deals completed through escrow and any diversion reports — nothing self-declared.
