Questions

The short answers.

Is Saviku a property management system, or a chatbot?

Both. Properties, availability, rates, restrictions and reservations all live in one PMS record per property, checked every minute and synced out to your connected channels. The 24/7 AI co-host is the WhatsApp conversation layered on top of it — not a separate product.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Saviku runs inside WhatsApp on the number you already use. A web page opens only for the few things a bigger screen does better — a document photo, a signature, a date grid — and it takes you straight back to the chat.

Is a bot answering me?

Savi is hybrid: deterministic automation handles structured actions and keeps every money or identity step on fixed, auditable paths; AI helps understand eligible free-text requests and route them to approved actions; and a human concierge steps in when context or judgement is needed. AI never invents payment, identity or release instructions.

Which channels can I connect?

Airbnb, Booking.com, Expedia and Vrbo connect through realtime two-way channel sync. Reservations flow into Saviku, and the availability, rates and restrictions (ARI) you set from WhatsApp flow back out — checked every minute, not on a scheduled calendar refresh.

Can two channels double-book the same night?

Saviku checks new bookings against dates already reserved and sends availability updates to connected channels. Separate channels can still accept bookings before an update arrives, so simultaneous bookings or sync delays can cause conflicts. Saviku flags conflicting incoming bookings for review; channel updates are not an instant guarantee.

How does the 48-Hour Booking Protection work?

Secure Booking: Your payment is processed through Saviku’s payment provider, allocated to your booking and not paid directly to the host at collection.

Protection Window: You have 48 hours after scheduled check-in to cancel or report a material problem.

Host Payout: If no eligible cancellation or dispute is open, Saviku instructs the provider to pay the verified host after that window, subject to provider availability.

Guest Guarantee: If any critical listing or access issues occur, you are covered under Saviku’s booking protection policy.

When do hosts receive their payout?

Predictable 48-Hour Payouts

Host payout is scheduled to your registered M-PESA or approved account 48 hours after scheduled check-in, minus Saviku's disclosed service fee, unless an eligible cancellation or dispute is open.

Can agents and property managers use it?

Yes. Agents follow the same verification ladder plus referee confirmations, and get a shareable profile that shows bookings completed through Saviku’s booking-protection flow and any diversion reports — nothing self-declared.
